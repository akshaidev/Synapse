PROPOSED SOLUTION

❖ Preemptive Golden-Hour ATM Interdiction & Cyber Fraud Interception
THE GOLDEN-HOUR PARADIGM: Over 85% of cyber fraud funds exit the formal banking perimeter via rapid ATM cash-outs within 120 minutes. Synapse predicts the specific physical ATM terminal before cash is withdrawn, executing automated bank digital liens and dispatching nearest tactical units.
🔍 DETAILED EXPLANATION
End-to-End Prediction Core
  • Terminal Mule Isolation: NetworkX DAG directed graph traversal isolates leaf node accounts across complex multi-hop fund layers.
  • Temporal Urgency Engine: Calculates remaining drainable balance against synthetic 4.5-min AMLSim withdrawal intervals.
  • Spatial ATM Ranking: Evaluates 200 Delhi ATMs via Haversine distance, bank affiliation, cash level, and accessibility.
  • Explainable Composite Score: Evaluates combined confidence to dictate automated intervention tiers.
⚡ ADDRESSING THE PROBLEM
Targeted Operational Impact
  • Physical Cash-Out Boundary: Bypasses circular online money laundering by intercepting the physical cash extraction point.
  • 120-Min Expiry Filter: Automatically gates out stale tickets, concentrating officer attention strictly on interceptable funds.
  • Victim Protection Guarantee: Strict mathematical whitelist ensures the reporting victim account is excluded from lien freezes.
  • Dual-Channel Interdiction: Intercepts digital funds via bank webhooks while positioning tactical police cordons.
💡 INNOVATION & UNIQUENESS
Novel Architectural Features
  • Confirmed ATM Telemetry: 3rd location source dynamically updates suspect coordinates to confirmed ATM terminal with green pulse beacon.
  • Tactical Cordon Mapping: Automates 800m outer containment perimeter and 300m inner arrest cordon on satellite maps.
  • Static Fallback Confidence Cap: Hard-caps coarse IP geolocations at 0.75 confidence to eliminate false tactical deployments.
  • Deterministic Drill Mode: Built-in Bank Feed Simulator enables live police academy training drills.

🛡 OPERATIONAL FEASIBILITY
Law Enforcement Integration
  • NCRP Complaint Compliant: Ingests standard National Cybercrime Reporting Portal tickets without schema changes.
  • Two-Tier Command UI: View A provides high-volume queue triage for controllers; View B provides tactical GPS maps for field units.
  • Integrated Drill Simulator: `/feed` simulator enables repeatable live cyber fraud response drills for officer training.
  • Lightweight Deployment: Runs in single Docker container on standard Linux servers without specialized GPU clusters.
⚠️ RISKS & MITIGATIONS
Engineering Safety Controls
  • Risk: Coarse IP Drift (±1-5km):
    Mitigation: Hard confidence cap at 0.75 prevents false tactical field deployments without verified telemetry.
  • Risk: Erroneous Freeze of Victims:
    Mitigation: Deterministic victim exclusion whitelist mathematically isolates the reporting victim from all lien controls.
  • Risk: High Ingestion Load:
    Mitigation: Clear architectural roadmap to PostgreSQL / TimescaleDB and Redis pub/sub queue.
